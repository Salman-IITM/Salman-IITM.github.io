01 — Focus

Research Interests

～

Wave propagation & scattering

How gravity waves disperse across rigid or flexible, permeable or impermeable obstructions in the domain.

▦

Porous & floating structures

Perforated and compartmentalized cages, pontoons, and barges for mariculture and as passive wave-attenuating systems.

≈

Experimental hydrodynamics

Physical model testing in wave flumes and wave basin to validate and verify concepts, using appropriate instrumentation and sophisticated contactless techniques .

∫

Numerical modelling of FSI

Fluid–structure interaction, boundary-element formulations, frequency domain and time-domain methods.
